## Findings

Composition: the sources agree a hole reads as a ribbon of mown green (tee, fairway, green) cut out of a rougher, more textured surround, with the pin and flag as the one vertical accent; the sea, or a dune wall, closes the background, and bunkers with rough shaggy lips frame the green. Camera (arcade games, from search snippets: Mario Golf reviews note a mostly overhead and behind-the-ball view, and a camera that follows the shot path; Hot Shots/Everybody's Golf use a behind-the-golfer view and a cut to a ball-follow cam): for a tilt-shift look, put the camera high and angled down at about 35-50 degrees with a narrow field of view, a blur band at the top and bottom of the frame, and a lifted-saturation, slightly contrasty grade so the island reads as a miniature; follow the ball and ease back to a view of the green. Lighting and colour: low warm sun with long soft shadows; strong green fairways against straw-gold dunes, pale sand, deep blue water, and a few red or white flag accents. Golf Story's 16-bit look is described as simple so that terrain can be read at a glance; the same holds here, with painterly flat colour patches and few hard outlines. Production design: nine short holes wrapped round a small island, a clubhouse, stone walls, split-rail fences, gorse and marram grass tufts, a lighthouse or stone hut as landmarks, pot bunkers, numbered tee markers; coastal links are treeless and rolling. Motion: the loop, as the series describes it, is aim, then three presses of one button (first starts the meter, second sets power, third sets accuracy, where the closer to the origin the straighter the shot), then the ball flies in an arc with wind drift, rolls, and a short result banner; sheep wander slowly and scatter lightly when the ball lands near. A wind flag flutters in proportion to wind. Sound: soft club thwack and a bright meter tick on each press, a pitched rising whine as the meter fills, wind and gull bed with surf, a soft cup rattle and a cheerful short jingle for birdie, and sheep bleats at a distance (from series descriptions, not heard). Motifs: flag on a pole, meter bar, wind arrow, sheep, bunker, dunes and sea. Materials: matte painted grass with mow-stripe banding, grainy sand, straw-coloured dune grass cards, grey stone, weathered wood; nothing glossy.
